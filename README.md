# Be Aware of Lava

A little 3D pixel platformer where the floor is, quite literally, lava. Land on platforms, keep climbing, and don't let the lava touch you. Simple idea, surprisingly stressful.

 Play it here: kabirutreja.itch.io/be-aware-of-la

Made by Kabir for PIXL ysws program. 

# What's the game?

You control a tiny pixel guy who has to keep jumping onto new platforms while the camera slowly drifts upward and the lava rises beneath you. Fall behind and you're toast.


# Controls

Move	Arrow keys / WASD

Jump	Space

Pause	Esc
